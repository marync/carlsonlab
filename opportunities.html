Opportunities

The lab will officially launch in January 2027, however, I am actively recruiting researchers at all levels.

Graduate students

The lab welcomes all students with a strong interest in quantitative biology. Prospective students are encouraged to apply through the Biology graduate program. Current and prospective students should contact Maryn to discuss potential research directions. Please include a brief description of your research interests and training, and a CV.

Postdocs

The lab is actively recruiting postdocs, with potential to start as early as January 2027. Please contact Maryn to discuss research opportunities. Please include a brief description of your research interests and training, and a CV.

Undergraduates

The lab has many research opportunities for undergraduate researchers, with potential projects in genomics, statistical methods development, population genetic theory, and data analysis. Please contact Maryn to discuss potential research projects. Please include a brief description of your academic interests and background.
