The Carlson Lab
Department of Biology
University of Rochester

The Carlson Lab works at the interface of population and quantitative genetics, statistics, and evolutionary biology. A major focus of the lab's current work is developing theory and methods for interpreting high-throughput mutagenesis studies, particularly in the context of within and across species variation. Work in the lab combines theory, methods development, and data analysis.

While the lab will not officially launch until January 2027, I am actively recruiting undergraduate researchers, graduate students, and postdocs. Please see Join the lab for opportunities to join the group.
